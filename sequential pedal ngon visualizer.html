Sequential Pedal Construction and Pivot Circles

Construction:
Starting with a simple n-gon \(P_0=(V_1^{(0)},\ldots,V_n^{(0)})\), each vertex of the next polygon is the perpendicular foot of the fixed point \(P\) on the corresponding edge of the previous polygon.

Pivot circles at the transition \(P_k\to P_{k+1}\):
\(C_1\) passes through \(V_1^{(k)},V_1^{(k+1)},V_n^{(k+1)}\), while \(C_i\) passes through \(V_i^{(k)},V_i^{(k+1)},V_{i-1}^{(k+1)}\), \(i=2,\ldots,n\).

The visualization highlights the fixed point \(P\). For nondegenerate configurations, the pivot circles pass through this same point.
